Kondobox

Declutter your photo library, one swipe at a time

How It Works

Kondobox shows you random photos from your library so you can quickly decide what to keep, save, favorite, or delete. Swipe through a batch of photos in each session and keep your memories organized without the overwhelm.

1

Start a Review

Tap "Start Review" to load a batch of random photos from your library. Choose to review 5, 10, 20, 50, or all of your photos at once.

2

Swipe to Decide

Each photo appears as a card. Swipe in one of four directions or tap the action buttons to decide what happens with each photo.

3

Stay on Track

Set up reminders to review your photos on a schedule. Kondobox will send you a gentle notification when it's time to tidy up.

Swipe Gestures

Four intuitive gestures to sort your photos quickly.

👉

Swipe Right — Save

Save the photo to your Kondobox album. Great for photos you want to print, share, or revisit.

👈

Swipe Left — Keep

Keep the photo as-is and move on. It won't appear again in future reviews.

👆

Swipe Up — Delete

Delete the photo from your library. You'll be asked to confirm before anything is removed.

👇

Swipe Down — Favorite

Mark the photo as a Favorite. Favorited photos are automatically skipped in future reviews.

Features

  • Swipe-based photo review with four actions: save, keep, delete, and favorite
  • Customizable session length — review 5, 10, 20, 50, or unlimited photos
  • Automatically skips photos you've already reviewed and your Favorites
  • Custom album name for saved photos
  • Configurable reminders from daily to weekly
  • Session stats and progress tracking with celebratory confetti
  • Photo metadata display including date, time, and location
  • Reset review history to start fresh anytime

Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

Your Photos Stay on Your Device

Kondobox works entirely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server, cloud service, or third party. All processing happens locally on your iPhone or iPad.

What We Access

  • Photo Library: Kondobox requests access to your photo library to display photos for review and to manage albums, favorites, and deletions. You can choose to grant full access or select specific photos.
  • Notifications: If you enable reminders, Kondobox schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent externally.

Data Storage

Kondobox stores your preferences (session length, album name, reminder frequency) and a list of reviewed photo identifiers locally on your device using standard system storage. This data is not shared with anyone and is removed if you delete the app.

No Analytics or Tracking

Kondobox does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools. We do not collect any usage data, personal information, or device identifiers.

No Network Requests

Kondobox does not make any network requests. The app functions entirely offline. The only network activity that may occur is Apple's own iCloud Photo Library syncing, which is managed by the system and not by Kondobox.

Children's Privacy

Kondobox does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not collect any data from any user.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

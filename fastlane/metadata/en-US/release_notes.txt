New, simpler swipe gestures:
- Swipe left to delete
- Swipe right to keep
- Swipe up to save to your album
- Swipe down to favorite

Clear arrow icons now show which way to swipe, and the overlay names the action as you swipe.

Fixed an issue where the review screen could appear cut off when Button Shapes or larger text sizes were turned on.

The gesture guide now lives in the Help tab.
